        📘 OEM Nomenclature, Service Notes & Technical Links

        1. York Series 100 IPU Service Access & Output Forcing

        • Service access: Press SERVICE, then enter the field access code (this simulator uses 1325 as recorded in the site notes — confirm the code for your unit's firmware in the IPU manual).

        • Forcing digital / analog outputs (DO/AO): Put the unit rocker switch in LOCAL STOP (OFF) first, then SERVICE → FORCE DO/AO, select the channel with ▲▼ and press ✓. Returning the switch to RUN (ON) cancels forced outputs.

        • Clearing hard lockouts (3 trips / 120 min): Fix the root cause, press the manual reset on HP1/HP2/HP3 or the Detail A HPS, then press RESET LOCKOUT on the IPU keypad.
